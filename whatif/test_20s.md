HERO stands still in the middle of the sidewalk as dozens of glowing pale-blue wisps of light stream from the heads of passersby and swirl around him like a storm; he presses both hands over his ears and squeezes his eyes shut. Camera: slow orbit around HERO, the crowd motion-blurred.
