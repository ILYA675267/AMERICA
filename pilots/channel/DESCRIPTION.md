# MiloLore — channel description (English)

Small animated stories about love, life & the little things.

Meet Milo — a tiny guy with a sprout on his head that grows when he's happy and wilts when he's not. Together with Mia, he goes through the things we all go through: falling in love, silent fights, long nights, small kindnesses and the moments nobody else sees.

Every story is a short animated film about the quiet side of relationships and everyday life — the small things that make us feel loved, the habits that slowly break us, and the ones that heal.
