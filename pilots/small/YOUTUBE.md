# YouTube upload — "The Small Things"

## Title
She Won't Remember the Gifts. She'll Remember This. | An Animated Short Film

## Description
She won't remember the expensive gifts. Forty years later, she'll remember the coffee you made just the way she likes it.

This is a short animated film about the little things in love — the ones nobody posts, nobody sees, and nobody forgets.

Milo isn't a hero. He doesn't plan grand gestures. He just remembers how she takes her coffee. He stays awake until she texts "I'm home". He warms her hands at a cold bus stop, covers her with a blanket when she falls asleep during a movie, runs out in the rain with an umbrella, stays by her side when she's sick, and leaves tiny notes on the fridge before work.

Seven small moments. A jar full of fireflies. A love that lasted forty years.

Because love isn't one big moment.
It's a thousand small ones nobody else sees. 🌱
